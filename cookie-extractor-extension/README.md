# Curvy Cookie Extractor Chrome Extension

A lightweight Chrome / Brave / Edge extension to extract session cookies from Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, and Meesho for Curvy Admin.

## How to Install (Takes 30 Seconds)

1. Open Google Chrome, Brave, or Microsoft Edge.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions` in your address bar.
3. Turn **ON** the **"Developer mode"** toggle in the top-right corner.
4. Click **"Load unpacked"** (top-left button).
5. Select this folder:
   `C:\Users\PC\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\curvy\cookie-extractor-extension`

Done! You will see the **Curvy Extractor** icon in your browser toolbar (click the puzzle icon to pin it).

---

## How to Use

### Method A: 1-Click Sync to Your Curvy Server (Recommended)
1. Click the Curvy Extractor icon in your browser toolbar.
2. In the "Direct Sync to Curvy Admin" box, enter your site URL (e.g. `https://your-domain.com` or `http://localhost:3000`).
3. Click **"🚀 1-Click Sync All Cookies to Server"**.
4. That's it! Your server now has fresh cookies for Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, etc. You never need to copy-paste cookies again!

### Method B: Copy & Paste Individual Cookies
1. Click the extension icon.
2. It detects cookies for Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, etc.
3. Click **"Copy"** next to any store.
4. Go to Curvy Admin `/admin/import` and paste it into the cookies box.
