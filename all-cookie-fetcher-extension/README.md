# 1-Click All Cookie Fetcher Extension

Fetch and copy all available cookies from Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, Meesho and more with a single click.

## How to Install (Takes 30 Seconds)

1. Open Google Chrome, Brave, or Microsoft Edge.
2. In your address bar, navigate to:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions
   ```
3. In the top-right corner, turn **Developer mode** **ON**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left corner.
5. Select this folder:
   ```text
   C:\Users\PC\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\curvy\all-cookie-fetcher-extension
   ```
6. The extension is now loaded! Pin it to your toolbar by clicking the puzzle icon in Chrome.

---

## How to Use

1. Click the **1-Click Cookie Fetcher** icon in your Chrome toolbar.
2. Click the big green button:
   **`⚡ FETCH & COPY ALL COOKIES`**
3. That's it! It copies all your active cookies for Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, and Meesho into your clipboard at once.
4. Go to your Curvy Admin (`/admin/import`), paste the copied content into the **Personal PC Cookies** box, and click **Fetch Data**.
   Curvy automatically detects each store and uses the right cookies for whatever link you paste!
